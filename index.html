About

Luisa is a Product Designer experienced in shaping complex digital platforms with clear structure, accessible interactions, and practical product thinking.

Her work connects Design Systems, UX Writing, WordPress, Information Architecture, and technical collaboration to help teams make digital experiences easier to use, maintain, and evolve.

Core Strengths

  • Systems Thinking

    Connecting user needs, product goals, and technical constraints into coherent experiences.

  • Design Systems

    Creating reusable patterns that help products scale with consistency and clarity.

  • Technical Collaboration

    Working closely with development teams to design solutions that can be built well.

  • Information Architecture

    Organizing content and flows so people can find, understand, and complete what matters.

  • UX Writing

    Using clear product language to guide users through complex decisions and tasks.

  • Problem Solving

    Breaking down ambiguity into focused design decisions and practical next steps.
