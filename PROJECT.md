The ultimate goal is to create opportunities for meaningful conversations and interviews.

---

# Design Philosophy

I believe great digital products happen where user needs, business goals and technical feasibility meet.

Every design decision should solve a problem.

Clarity is more valuable than decoration.

Design systems create consistency and scalability.

Documentation is part of the design process.

Accessibility should be considered from the beginning, not at the end.

Good collaboration produces better products.
